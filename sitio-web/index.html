Clean & sanitization

What We Clean

Kitchen & Bath

  • Wipe and clean all cabinets, including interior shelving and drawers
  • Clean all mirrors, sinks, toilets, showers/tubs and towel bars
  • Clean countertops
  • Pull out appliances and clean inside and out: refrigerator/freezer, oven and stove (burners removed and cleaned, drip pans), washer/dryer
  • Microwave and dishwasher cleaned in place (not removed)

Doors & Windows

  • Clean all interior windows, including tracks
  • Wipe all blinds (dust removed)
  • Remove cobwebs from the outside front door; sweep the entry area and balcony floors
  • Clean and scrub vinyl floors and baseboards as needed
  • Vacuum all carpeting and mop all floors
  • Clean light fixtures, exhaust fans, heating vents and outlet covers

Heavy cleaning has an additional charge depending on the condition of the apartment.
