Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

When you submit a cleaning request, we collect your full name, phone number, the service you need, the property address, your preferred date and any optional details you choose to add.

How we use it

Samyara Clean LLC uses this information only to respond to your request: to contact you by text message, discuss the details, and confirm availability. A request is not a confirmed booking.

Text messages and marketing

We will use your phone number to reach you about your request. We do not use it for marketing campaigns or promotional messages unless you separately agree to receive them. Submitting a request is not consent to advertising.

Who sees your information

Your request is stored securely and is visible only to Samyara Clean LLC. We use service providers to run the website, store requests and send text-message notifications to our team. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and your choices

We keep requests only as long as needed to serve you and meet our business needs. You can ask us to correct or delete your information at any time by replying to the text message we send you.
